Section 05b

End-to-End Encryption

As of June 2026, EzraAI encrypts sensitive personal data stored in our database using AES-256-GCM end-to-end encryption. This means your conversation history and personal memories are encrypted before they are written to storage, and remain unreadable to anyone who does not hold the master decryption secret — including in the event of a database breach.

What is encrypted

  • Your full conversation history with EzraAI (per server and in DMs)
  • Personal memory facts you share with the bot (e.g. your name, preferences)
  • Your sentiment history used for wellbeing and vibe features

What is not encrypted

  • Economy data such as XP, coins, level, aura, and badges — these remain plaintext so leaderboards and dashboards function correctly
  • Server configuration settings — these contain no personal conversation content
  • Your Discord User ID — this is used as an identifier and cannot be encrypted

How it works

A unique 256-bit encryption key is derived for each individual user using PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations, SHA-512) from a master secret stored exclusively as a server environment variable — it is never written to the database. Each encryption operation uses a random 96-bit IV and produces a 128-bit GCM authentication tag, ensuring both confidentiality and tamper-detection.

When EzraAI generates a reply, your conversation history is temporarily decrypted in-memory on our server. This is necessary to provide the Service. Decrypted content is never written to disk or logs and is discarded immediately after the reply is sent.

Security implications for you

  • If our database were ever compromised, your conversation history and memories would be unreadable without the master secret
  • Each user's key is derived independently — a breach affecting one user cannot be used to decrypt another user's data
  • Requesting deletion of your data also invalidates your derived key, making any residual encrypted data permanently unreadable
  • Attempting to probe, extract, or circumvent the encryption system is a violation of Section 4 and will result in a permanent ban
